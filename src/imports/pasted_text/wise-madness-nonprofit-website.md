Design a modern, bold, and impactful nonprofit website for "Associação Wise Madness" — a Brazilian social organization founded in 2006 in Bauru, SP. The name means "Wise Madness" (Sábia Loucura) — balancing wisdom with daring action.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Primary colors: Deep Red (#CC0000), Black (#111111), White (#FFFFFF)
- Accent: Light gray (#F5F5F5) for section backgrounds
- Typography: Montserrat or Inter — Bold (700–800) for headings, Regular (400) for body
- Tone: Inspiring, human, community-driven, bold but warm
- Logo: geometric V+W symbol in red, wordmark in black — place prominently in header

DESIGN STYLE GUIDELINES:
- Use strong typographic hierarchy: massive hero headlines, clear section titles, readable body text
- Red accent color used intentionally: CTAs, card top bars, numbers, active states
- Generous whitespace — sections breathe, not cluttered
- Photography/image placeholders: children playing, community, art activities (warm, real, not stock-y)
- Mobile-first responsive: hero stacks vertically, cards go single column
- Avoid: generic charity aesthetics, pale pastels, stock photo feel
- Feel: bold social impact brand — closer to a movement than a traditional NGO website
- Navigation: sticky on scroll, logo left, menu right, red "Apoiar" button always visible
